What phisor has learned

Night by night

Each night adds new text, picks up from the last saved night and is scored on the guide's held-out sentences, whose labels it is never told. A line that climbs means the learning carried over. Questions and instructions are sensed by the kinds named for them on dev: by lift, each kind names the label it holds the largest share of (recorded from 27 November); by dev majority, the label most of its dev sentences carry, which never names a rare label like the guide's instructions.

Word classes

The word code, grown as deep as the text pays for. Neighbouring cards share their first bits, so they are close in the tree. Words in blue are a word's opening sense: the word as the first of a sentence.

Kinds of sentence

Sentence templates grouped by the class pairs their sentences hold. The bars show how the guide's held-out sentences in each kind are labelled: what they are for (explain, instruct, answer) and how they end (stop, question, exclamation, line).
