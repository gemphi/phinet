Phinet in three dimensions

In Phient's world a word is a seed of what it relates to, under what conditions. Eight words from the Austen voice lexicon (data/austen_voice.chroma), real phasors, real evidence.

Words

Pair reading

Chord colour = on(a,b,k) = cos(src_a − tgt_b) on each evidenced channel: green agrees, red repels. First selected word is a, second is b.

Channel

Each word's phasor on this channel, stereographically lifted: unit magnitude sits on the equator; evidence lifts it toward the north pole (phi_cal::sphere::lift). Blue = source side, orange = target side.

Hexagram (bottom line first)

Six lines gate six octaves of the word's target-side spectrum. The ring is the function (height = Re, colour = Im); the inner ring is the gated function. phinet::hexagram::apply.

Modes kept

One Fourier layer on the word's function: F⁻¹(R·F h) with a random spectral filter R restricted to the kept modes. Outer ring: input. Inner ring: output. Fewer modes = smoother over the circle — the regulariser of the FNO.
